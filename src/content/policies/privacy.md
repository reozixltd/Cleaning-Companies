## What we collect

When you book, ask for a walk-through, join the waitlist or apply for a job, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone, email, address and the notes you add. For bookings we also keep access notes, such as a lockbox code.

## Why we use it

- To quote, confirm and do your clean, and to send messages about it: confirmation, a reminder the day before, an on-the-way text and a review request afterwards.
- To send offers, but only if you ticked the marketing box for SMS or email. Those boxes start unticked, and you can say no to one and yes to the other.

## Who sees it

Your cleaner and our office team. We don't sell or share your details. Our booking and messaging tools store data on our behalf.

## Changing your mind

Reply STOP to any marketing text, or reply "unsubscribe" to any marketing email. We stop within 5 business days. Booking messages about a clean you've booked aren't marketing, so they keep coming while you're a client.

## Seeing or correcting your details

Email or call us and we'll show you what we hold, fix it, or delete it if we no longer need it.
